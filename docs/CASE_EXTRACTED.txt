The Business Challenge
Warehouse leaders make staffing decisions in an
environment where demand can change based on:
• Day of the week
• Customer traffic
• Holidays and promotions
• Weather
• Warehouse size
• Department activity
• Employee availability and qualifications
• Callouts and overtime requirements
Too much labor increases operating costs. Too little labor can
result in longer wait times, slower replenishment, reduced
product availability, and lower member satisfaction.

Costco needs a practical way to balance labor, staffing
coverage, and service levels.

The Decision Costco Needs to Make

Your team must help Costco answer:
How should the warehouse allocate labor across departments and time periods to meet
expected demand at the lowest practical cost while maintaining service levels?

Your plan must cover all three scenarios in the planning week (Feb 12-18, 2026):
• Baseline: the expected week, including Valentine’s Day and Presidents’ Day
• Peak Demand: a promotion launch and a winter-storm advisory that drive extra traffic
• Disruption: illness and crew shortages that reduce available staff

Your recommendation should show where, when, and how much labor is needed, which
assumptions drive it, and what the warehouse leader should do when conditions shift.

Solution Expectations
Build a proof of concept, not just a dashboard. Any working
tool a warehouse leader could use qualifies.

Your solution should demonstrate how Costco could use data to
make better staffing decisions.

A dashboard may be included as part of the solution, but it
should not be the solution by itself. Teams should go beyond
displaying metrics and demonstrate how their approach:
• Forecasts or estimates future demand
• Converts demand into staffing requirements
• Recommend schedules, staffing levels, or labor coverage
• Balances labor cost with service-level objectives
• Responds to changing conditions or scenarios
• Explains why a recommendation was made
