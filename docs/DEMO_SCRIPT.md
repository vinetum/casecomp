# Prompt 1 functional demonstration

Launch with `./scripts/start.sh`. The **Start guided demo** button changes real selections and calls the API; no numerical result is canned.

1. **BASE:** Warehouse 2, Feb 14, MID. Read the official weekly cards, then inspect the selected block's draft and optimized headcount. Explain that counts are block assignments and service is modeled probability.
2. **PEAK:** Next demo scene switches the official scenario. Show exact promotion/storm multipliers' impact; inspect the real returned counts rather than quoting preliminary research values.
3. **DISRUPT:** Next scene uses illness-adjusted capacity. Show binding constraints, remaining risk and the non-executable draft warning. Lower availability or raise demand in Scenario Lab, click Recalculate, then restore official assumptions. Zero availability demonstrates an honest minimum-staff conflict with zero invented workers.
4. **The Last Worker:** Final scene selects Warehouse 2, Feb 13, OPEN, DISRUPT. Under Service First, a positive one-worker improvement may not exist: say so. Switch to Balanced in Staffing Optimizer, then return to Decision & Export to compare eligible additions and transfers with lender opportunity cost. Never force a transfer or claim a causal effect.
5. Inspect frontier alternatives, the Feasibility Certificate and sensitivity estimates. Explain that standalone maxima are not jointly additive.
6. Enter the team ID and download the official 567-row CSV. The download is independently verified and intentionally uses unmodified official scenarios, independent of custom simulation controls.

`artifacts/presentation_evidence.json` includes a programmatically selected largest scenario staffing divergence from actual optimized outputs; it currently points to Warehouse 2 / Feb 14 / MID / Front End. The exact outputs are recalculated on every export. Screenshots in `artifacts/screenshots` are from the running app, generated by `scripts/browser_smoke.py`. This is the Prompt 1 acceptance demo, not execution of Prompt 3.
